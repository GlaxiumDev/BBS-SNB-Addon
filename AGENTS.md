# Agent Instructions

## Communication style
- Before running any non-trivial shell command or starting a multi-step task, state briefly in 1-2 lines what you are about to do and why (e.g. "Let me check how X works first...").
- For big tasks, narrate progress as you go: a short status line before each major step (e.g. "Checked that. Now looking at how Y works..."). Do not narrate for trivial actions.
- After completing a big task, give a short 2-4 line summary of what was done and the outcome.
- Keep answers concise for simple questions and small edits; the narration rule applies to commands and substantial work only.
